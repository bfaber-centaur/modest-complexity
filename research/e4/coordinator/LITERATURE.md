## What this means for e4 assignments

- **Dynamics (decay strength).** Varma et al. predict a delay without prevention, and a decay-independent end state at convergence. A 20k window at wd 0.1 (exposure 2.0) may end before the wd-1 collapse exposure of about 1–5, so "delay" and "persistence" may be indistinguishable there. The lane states this before running.
- **Mechanisms (what changes).** The cheapest borrowed diagnostics are from Janati et al.: a training-pairs-only linear decoder on h, readout/feature swaps between declared states, and an additive logit split. Each needs our own definitions; none presumes their mechanism.
- **Audit.** Distinguish three things: weight filtering (ours, gate-changing), activation projection (Janati et al.), and descriptive Fourier projection of the logit table.
