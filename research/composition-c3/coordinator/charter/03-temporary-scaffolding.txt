ROLE: TEMPORARY-SCAFFOLDING INVESTIGATOR
Question: after learning with intermediate-state supervision, does composition persist when training continues with only final-answer supervision?

Use the tied-transition/tied-readout architecture throughout. Architecture, task, split, width, batches, learning rate and decay stay fixed. Coordinator selects common fixed switch times from existing c2 trajectories before c3 results (suggest 1k and 10k). Do not choose per-seed switch points by held-out success. If an event-based switch is ever used, specify it as an adaptive policy and use training-only criteria.

For each seed and switch time, branch from exactly the same full-state checkpoint:
A. Keep weights on the three losses at [1,1,1].
B. Drop intermediate losses immediately to [0,0,1].
C. Linearly taper the two intermediate weights from 1 to 0 over a fixed declared duration (suggest 2k updates), keeping final weight at 1.
D. Final-only from initialization, at the same TOTAL training horizon; reuse the exact c2 trajectory if compatible.
Use one shared unchanged A trajectory per seed where possible rather than duplicate it for every switch. Also retain the no-training checkpoint evaluation at each switch.

Carry optimizer moments and step in every main branch. A zero intermediate-loss weight does not freeze shared parameters: they still get final-loss gradients, decay and carried momentum. Explicitly test the weight schedule and gradient accounting. Hold the final loss coefficient at 1; report total loss scaling and effective label exposure. More labels are part of this intervention, not a confound that can be claimed away. Abrupt removal mixes loss-direction and loss-magnitude changes with optimizer-history effects.

Use a fixed total endpoint suggested at 50k, calibrated before launch. Evaluate original unseen length-three programs, the non-neighbour subset and frozen lengths 4–6. At lengths 1–2 distinguish previously supervised states from novel generalization. Never select checkpoints by the best held-out score; show trajectories at fixed times. Mark near-perfect starting seeds and partial starting seeds individually.

Define retention as changes relative to each branch point and the matched continued-supervision arm. Predeclare a descriptive degradation threshold (e.g. five percentage points at the endpoint), while reporting full curves so the threshold does not replace the data. For early switches also report acquisition, not just retention. Performance collapse, persistence and further improvement are all useful results.

First figure: trajectories aligned at the switch plus performance at equal total updates, with length-three and longer-length panels. Plot the supervision schedule. Include per-seed values, train loss and practical optimizer diagnostics.

One follow-up only if needed: matched reset-versus-carried moments in BOTH keep and drop arms, or one explicitly justified loss-scale control. Do not reset only the drop arm and attribute the difference to removal of supervision. Any changed loss scale can change the decay balance and Adam epsilon regime; report it.

Interpretation: lasting benefit supports a training-history effect under this recipe. It does not show discovery without extra information, universal modularity, or a stable asymptote. Loss of longer-program performance with stable length-three accuracy is a meaningful separate outcome.
